## Reading it

- **Overall:** AutoETS and AutoARIMA beat the random walk by about 10–17%. Most of that
  comes from the trending, smooth macro series (CPI, gold, the S&P 500 level) and from the
  return series.
- **Market prices** (Brent, WTI, EUR, JPY, VIX, natural gas): nothing beats the random
  walk. The Auto* models collapse to it, which is the expected efficient-market result and
  the real test for Timestral.
- **`sp500_monthly_logret`:** repeating last month's return (`naive`) is a poor forecast.
  `historic_average` (0.633) is the right reference for returns, and the Auto* models
  roughly match it.
- **Calibration:** only AutoETS's 80% band is calibrated (0.80). AutoARIMA's and Theta's
  are too narrow.
- **Absolute MASE** on `sp500_monthly` is inflated by 1871-era prices. Compare across
  models only.
- **Sample size:** 6 windows at the series end is a small, recent-regime sample.
