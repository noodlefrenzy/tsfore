**Pairs:** Brent ↔ WTI, EUR ↔ JPY (both per USD), and the S&P 500 level and log returns
with VIX (monthly mean). Each pair is inner-joined on dates, and every model, including
the univariate ones, sees the same truncated target. `+cov` passes the companion as a
past covariate; `+joint` forecasts it jointly with the target. Only the target is scored.

## Reading it

- **Update (2026-10-18), 36-window rerun, VERIFIED** (`pairs-36w-2026-10-18.md`):
  - *Companions barely matter:* holds. Chronos-2-small+cov is again the only
    detectable gain (p = 0.017 on 216 windows).
  - *Every model improves with VIX on the S&P 500 level:* does not hold. Chronos-2 is
    unchanged (1.251), and Timestral-28M gets worse (1.179 → 1.197).
  - *AutoETS is the only well-calibrated model:* it still over-covers slightly (0.82).
    Timestral sits at 0.76–0.78 on this suite.
- **Companions barely matter here.** For Timestral the effect is noise: 209M gains
  0.3% in rel_MASE, and 28M loses 1.3%. Only Chronos-2-small gains detectably (about
  4% rel_MASE, p = 0.015). That result doesn't survive a Bonferroni correction across
  the five comparisons (about 0.075).
- **Chronos-2 benefits more than Timestral,** which fits its design around group
  attention. It still doesn't catch up: the best Chronos variant (small+cov, 0.955)
  trails univariate Timestral-209M (0.927).
- **The clearest per-series case is the S&P 500 level with VIX.** Every model improves
  with VIX: Timestral-209M by 3% (1.367 → 1.326, the best score in the row), Chronos-2
  by 10% and Chronos-2-small by 12.5%. Market-implied volatility carries some
  information about next year's average index level. VIX adds nothing to log returns,
  which all models already forecast at about 0.67.
- **Oil and FX** stay at random-walk level with or without the companion. Brent and WTI
  co-move on the same day, so yesterday's WTI tells you little about tomorrow's Brent
  that yesterday's Brent doesn't. Chronos-2-small+cov on EUR (0.986) is the only result
  below the random walk in the FX rows.
- **Timestral's lead over AutoETS disappears on this suite.** They tie at 0.927. The
  `finance` suite's gap came mostly from CPI, gold and VIX, which are not targets here,
  and this suite is mostly near-random-walk prices over the most recent 12 windows.
- **Chronos-2's `+cov` and `+joint` are the same computation.** At inference, Chronos-2
  places past-only covariates in the target's attention group with their future masked
  (`chronos2/dataset.py`), exactly as it treats extra targets. A smoke test confirmed
  identical outputs, so `+joint` is omitted. Timestral encodes covariates separately,
  so its two modes differ.
- **Calibration:** adding companions narrows Chronos-2's bands further (80% coverage
  0.72 → 0.66). AutoETS remains the only well-calibrated model (0.81).
- **Caveats:**
  - Six targets, of which four are market prices.
  - The S&P pairs have only 441 monthly points (VIX starts in 1990).
  - Timings include each model's first-call weight load, so compare them within this
    report only.
