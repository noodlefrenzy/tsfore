## What each window covers

Windows are the last `48 × horizon` steps. Daily series (horizon 20) cover about Dec 2022
to Oct 2026. Monthly series (horizon 12) cover Aug 1978 to Sep 2026, and gold's first
window has only 93 months of context.

## Reading it

- **Timestral leads, but less than 6 windows suggested.** rel_MASE 0.848–0.852 for
  28M, 123M and 209M (they are indistinguishable, p = 0.35), against 0.893 for AutoETS
  and 0.890 for Chronos-2. 5M is reliably worse than 209M (p = 5e-4).
- **Its point-forecast lead over AutoETS is mostly natural gas.** Natgas rel_MASE is
  0.639 for Timestral-209M against 1.020 for AutoETS. Without natgas the two tie
  (0.876 against 0.881), and the window-level test is not significant (p = 0.12).
- **The probabilistic lead is broader.** Without natgas, rel_WQL is still 0.789 for
  Timestral-209M against 0.821 for AutoETS. Timestral's WQL is better on gold, the
  S&P 500 level, VIX and the 10-year yield.
- **Calibration:** Timestral's 80% bands cover 0.80–0.81 on average, the best of any
  model. AutoETS over-covers (0.84, and 0.94 on natgas, so its bands are too wide).
  Chronos-2 under-covers (0.78). AutoARIMA collapses on the S&P 500 level (0.26).
- **Chronos-2 matches Timestral on natgas (0.644) and VIX, and beats it on the
  10-year yield (0.944 against 0.997).** It loses 3–5% to the random walk on Brent,
  WTI and EUR, where Timestral and AutoETS stay at 1.00. That overreaction on
  random-walk series is most of its gap to Timestral.
- **Nobody beats the random walk on oil or FX.** Every competent model sits at
  0.99–1.01 rel_MASE on Brent, WTI, EUR and JPY.
- **CPI is where statistics still wins on points.** AutoETS reaches 0.431 rel_MASE,
  Timestral-209M 0.458 and Chronos-2 0.681.
- **Caveat: pretraining leakage.** The monthly windows run from 1978. Both foundation
  models were trained on large public corpora that very likely include CPI, the
  S&P 500 and gold, so their monthly scores may partly reflect memorization. The daily
  windows (2023–2026) are less exposed, but neither model's training cutoff has been
  checked against them.
