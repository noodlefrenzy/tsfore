## Reading it

- **36 windows, not 48.** The S&P 500 + VIX pairs start in 1990 (VIX's first month)
  and have 441 points, room for 36 windows of 12. Their first window has only 9 months
  of context, which every model shares. The daily pairs cover Nov 2023 to Oct 2026.
- **Companions still don't help Timestral.** No variant moves rel_MASE by more than
  1%, and no paired test comes near significance.
- **Chronos-2-small+cov is the one durable effect.** p = 0.017 again, now on three
  times the windows (it was 0.015 on 72). With six comparisons, Bonferroni puts it at
  about 0.10. It doesn't close the gap: small+cov (0.962) still trails univariate
  Timestral-28M (0.932).
- **VIX helps the S&P 500 level only for some models.** Chronos-2-small improves 8.5%
  (1.328 → 1.215) and Timestral-209M 1.7% (1.192 → 1.172). Chronos-2 is unchanged
  (1.251) and Timestral-28M gets worse (1.179 → 1.197).
- **Statistics tie the foundation models on this suite.** AutoARIMA (0.926),
  Timestral-28M (0.932) and AutoETS (0.933) are within 1%. This suite has no natgas
  and no CPI, the two series behind most of Timestral's `finance` lead.
