# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS with no build step, served from GitHub Pages once the repo goes public (the human's plan, 2026-10-18). The page must work as plain files from a subdirectory.

## Users

Technical peers who know forecasting or ML: they read MASE, WQL, coverage and p-values without a glossary, and want the caveats in full. They arrive wanting to know whether a new time-series foundation model is worth trying on their own data.

## Product Purpose

tsfore is a playground that evaluates time-series foundation models (Mistral's Timestral, Amazon's Chronos-2) against classical statistical baselines with rolling-origin backtests. The site reports what those tests found: which family wins where, by how much, how sure we are, and what has not been tested yet. Success is a reader who leaves with an accurate, calibrated picture, including where the result is weak.

## Positioning

Small and honest beats big and glossy. Every number traces to a committed CSV and report in `docs/benchmarks/`, paired significance tests sit beside averages, and claims that later runs overturned are shown as corrected, not erased.

## Operating Context

Results come from `tsfore bench` runs on an RTX 4090 (WSL2). Reports live in `docs/benchmarks/` and are regenerated by rerunning the CLI. Readers may follow links from the site to those reports and to the code.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Three model families: classical statistics (naive, AutoETS, AutoARIMA, historic average), Timestral (5M, 28M, 123M, 209M) and Chronos-2 (small 28M, base 120M).
- Two suites: `finance` (11 public series, 8 committed and the S&P 500/VIX fetched on use; 48 windows) and `pairs` (6 target/companion pairs, 36 windows), plus companion modes `+cov` and `+joint`.
- Zero-shot only; no fine-tuning has been run.
- License: MIT for tsfore's code (`LICENSE`). Third-party terms are in `NOTICE.md`, and the site footer states both.

## Evidence on Hand

- `docs/benchmarks/finance-48w-2026-10-18.md`, `docs/benchmarks/pairs-36w-2026-10-18.md`, and the earlier 6/12-window reports with dated update notes.
- `docs/benchmarks/batching-2026-10-18.md` for runtime.
- Raw per-window CSVs are regenerable but gitignored (`results/`).
- No external users, testimonials or third-party benchmarks of this project exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Every figure is traceable to a committed report.
2. Show uncertainty next to the effect: averages with paired tests, wins with losses.
3. Corrections stay visible.
4. Name the limits (suite size, leakage, zero-shot) as prominently as the wins.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Charts need text equivalents (tables or labelled values), and color can't be the only carrier of meaning.
